NavFusion

AI for Robust Navigation Solutions

Tackling the critical vulnerability of GNSS reliance, NavFusion fuses cost-effective MEMS IMUs with advanced deep learning to provide continuous, accurate localization for mobile robots and vehicles when satellite signals are jammed, spoofed, or obstructed.

The Problem & Our Solution

Bridging the gap between low-cost sensors and high-grade reliability

As autonomous systems scale, the threat of GNSS interference grows. NavFusion provides a drop-in edge solution that leverages AI to predict and correct inertial drift—keeping vehicles on track without the prohibitive cost of tactical-grade IMUs.

Combatting GPS Denial

Designed for mobile ground and aerial robots that require continuous tracking through urban canyons, tunnels, and spoofing attacks.

Deep Learning Integration

Utilizes advanced PSO-optimized LSTM and GRU networks to learn complex motion dynamics and eliminate compounding IMU errors.

Proven Edge Performance

Features a custom-engineered, battery-powered sensor suite integrating a Movella MTi-3 and u-blox GNSS, rigorously tested on real-world trajectories.
