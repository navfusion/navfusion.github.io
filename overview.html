Project Overview

Mission and Background

NavFusion focuses on using AI-enhanced inertial navigation to improve performance in environments where GPS is denied, jammed, spoofed, or otherwise unreliable.

Background

Mobile robots typically depend on GNSS and exteroceptive sensors for localization, but these methods become difficult to rely on in environments with no reference points or poor signal availability. MEMS IMUs are cost-effective and compact, but they suffer from drift over time.

Project Scope

  • Study methods to overcome GPS signal loss.
  • Investigate AI-based solutions for inertial navigation improvement.
  • Evaluate neural networks, filters, and predictive approaches.
  • Deploy and demonstrate the system on a physical platform.

Use Case

Intended Application

The system is designed for ground or aerial vehicles that usually rely on GPS, but need a more robust solution during temporary GPS outages. It is meant to complement GPS rather than replace it.
